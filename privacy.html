Privacy Policy

DotcomRide collects personal information such as name, phone number, email address, and trip details solely for the purpose of providing private transportation services.

Phone numbers are used only to send transactional SMS messages related to ride confirmations, scheduling updates, and trip completion notices. DotcomRide does not sell, share, or rent personal information to third parties.

SMS consent is obtained at the time of booking. Users may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.

Contact: support@dotcomride.com
