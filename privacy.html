DotcomRide (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides private luxury transportation services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and booking services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Full name

We collect only the information necessary to provide our transportation services.

3. How We Use Your Information

4. SMS Communications

By providing your phone number through our booking form, you consent to receive SMS messages from DotcomRide related to your ride request.

Message types include:

  • Ride confirmation
  • Driver acceptance notification
  • Arrival updates
  • Ride completion confirmation
  • Customer service responses

Message frequency varies per ride request. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance.
