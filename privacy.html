Privacy Policy

Last Updated: [Feb.14,2026]

1. Introduction

DotcomRide (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides private luxury transportation services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and booking services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Pickup and drop-off locations
  • Ride date and time
  • Payment preference
  • Ride history

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Confirm and manage ride bookings
  • Send ride status updates (confirmation, driver acceptance, arrival, completion)
  • Provide customer support and service communications
  • Maintain ride history records
  • Send occasional promotional offers if you opt in (e.g., discounts, specials)
  • Improve service quality

We do not sell your personal information.

4. SMS Communications (Transactional + Marketing)

By providing your phone number through our booking form, you consent to receive SMS messages from DotcomRide. Messages may include transactional ride updates and, if you opt in, occasional promotional offers.

Message types may include:

  • Ride confirmation
  • Driver acceptance and arrival updates
  • Ride completion confirmation
  • Customer support responses
  • Occasional promotional offers and discounts (opt-in)

Message frequency: varies (typically a few messages per ride; promotional messages are occasional).
Message & data rates: may apply.
Opt out: reply STOP at any time.
Help: reply HELP for assistance.
Consent: consent is not a condition of purchase.

5. Payments

DotcomRide may use third-party payment processors (such as Square). We do not store your full card details on our servers.

6. Data Retention

We retain booking and rider information for operational, legal, and recordkeeping purposes, including ride history and customer support.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party providers including Supabase (database), Square (payments), Twilio (SMS), and Google Maps services (location/routing). These providers process information necessary to deliver our service.
